How I work

“What’s the point?”
It’s where I start.

Before getting into features or a delivery plan, I want to understand why the work needs doing. Who is having a problem? What are they trying to achieve? What would make their day easier? Those questions help me work out what to build, what to prioritise and whether the result is useful.

I’m a Salesforce Certified Administrator with 9+ years across consulting, product leadership and CRM delivery. Most recently, at Two Circles, I worked across five professional sport clients on discovery, proposals, training, integration investigation and migration workflows for 30,000+ historical CRM records.

My earlier career includes quality and impact work at Sheffield Futures, a third-sector charity, and records and compliance work at PeoplePlus/A4e and Irwin Mitchell. That background informs how I think about evidence, governance and the people using a service.

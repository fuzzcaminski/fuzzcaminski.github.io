Behind the camera

Paul Fores.
Otherwise, fuzzkamera.

I’m Paul, a photographer based in Sheffield, sharing my work as fuzzkamera.

I photograph people and the places we make, use and move through. Sometimes it’s someone glimpsed between machinery, sometimes an empty stadium or the strangeness of roadworks outside a bedroom window. I’m interested in how familiar places can feel different depending on where you stand and what’s happening—or has stopped happening—within them.

I work with film and digital cameras, often in black and white. Reflections, shapes and unexpected arrangements draw me into a photograph, but the surroundings matter too: where something belongs, how it’s used, and the traces people leave behind.

I try to bring brevity and humour into my photographs and their titles where appropriate, while always respecting the dignity of the people I photograph. I want to leave room for the feeling in a moment as well, the vulnerability or poignancy that can sit alongside its humour.

These are the things I’ve stopped to look at. I hope you find something in them worth stopping for too.
